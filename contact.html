Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

How often should I get my bins cleaned?

We recommend cleaning your bins every 4 weeks to maintain hygiene and prevent odors. However, you can also book one-off cleans as needed.

Do I need to be home when you clean my bins?

No, you don't need to be home! Simply leave your bins accessible and we'll take care of the rest. We'll send you a notification when the clean is complete.

Are your cleaning products eco-friendly?

Yes! We use environmentally friendly, biodegradable cleaning products that are safe for your family, pets, and the environment.

What if I need to cancel or rebook?

Simply give us a call on 07777 777777 and we'll be happy to reschedule your appointment. We ask for at least 24 hours notice for cancellations.

Do you clean commercial bins too?

Absolutely! We provide bin cleaning services for both residential and commercial customers. Contact us for a custom quote for your business.
