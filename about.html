About BinKind

Your local bin cleaning service, making life cleaner, easier, and kinder to the environment across Yeovil and Somerset

Our Story

A local business built on care, quality, and community

BinKind was founded with a simple belief: even the most overlooked tasks deserve to be done with care and respect. We saw that bins—something every home needs—were often neglected, leading to unpleasant odours, bacteria buildup, and an eyesore on otherwise tidy properties.

We knew there had to be a better way. That's why we started BinKind: to provide a professional, reliable, and environmentally responsible bin cleaning service right here in our local community. We're not a faceless national chain—we're your neighbours, committed to making everyday life a little bit easier and a lot more pleasant for everyone in Yeovil and the surrounding areas.

Today, we're proud to serve homes across South Somerset, delivering the kind of service we'd want for ourselves: thorough, dependable, and genuinely kind.
